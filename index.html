Privacy Policy

Mytools · Last updated: October 29, 2026

Mytools ("the App") is a personal tool that lets its owner upload and schedule videos and Reels to Facebook Pages they manage, using the official Facebook Graph API. This policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used.

1. Information the App accesses

When you connect your Facebook account through Facebook Login, the App may access:

  • The list of Facebook Pages you manage (Page ID and Page name) – permission pages_show_list;
  • A Page Access Token for each Page you choose, used to publish content on that Page – permission pages_manage_posts;
  • Basic Page information needed to confirm that publishing succeeded – permission pages_read_engagement;
  • The video files and captions you choose to upload through the App.

The App does not access your personal profile content, friends list, private messages, or any data of other Facebook users.

2. How the information is used

The information is used only to publish, schedule, list, or delete videos and Reels on the Pages you select, at your request. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

3. Storage and sharing

The App runs on the owner's own computer. Page IDs, Page Access Tokens, and publishing history are stored in local files on that computer. We do not operate a server that collects or stores your data. Video files are sent directly from your computer to Facebook and are only kept temporarily during upload.

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties. The only party that receives data from the App is Facebook (Meta), in order to perform the actions you request. Facebook's handling of that data is governed by Meta's Privacy Policy.

4. Data retention and deletion

Data stays on your computer until you remove it. You can delete your data at any time:

  • In the App, open the Pages tab and remove the Page (this deletes its stored token);
  • Delete the local files config.json and history.json;
  • Revoke the App's access in Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites), find Mytools, and choose Remove.

5. Data deletion requests

Because we do not store your data on any server, removing the App from your Facebook settings and deleting the local files fully removes your data. If you would like to confirm this or have any question, email us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.

6. Security

You are responsible for keeping the computer running the App, and the files config.json (which contains tokens), secure. Do not share these files.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
